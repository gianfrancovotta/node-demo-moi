How it works

Four steps to scale

You keep your recipe and your patents. We ask only for the data on how your process runs.

01

Apply

Pick a pilot plant and tell us about your technology. Then two meetings: one on the business, one technical with the team that would use it. We take the time to understand your idea well.

02

Test in our pilots

Run your process in a pilot plant with a NEXTCHEM engineer as your mentor. You pay with process data, not with money.

03

Get validated

You get a results report signed by NODE, an AI forecast of how your process works at full scale, and the NODE Validated badge to show investors.

Pilot plants

Test it big before you build it

A pilot plant is a small version of an industrial plant. It shows whether a process that works with a few grams in the lab still works with kilos or tonnes, before anyone invests millions in a full plant.

Find a pilot plant
  1. ChooseBrowse the plants by centre or by sector, or let the AI suggest one.
  2. BookApply online and meet our team on the business and on the technology. MAIRE signs the NDA first.
  3. RunYour test runs for weeks or months, with our engineers and lab analyses included.

Stories

Built at NODE

CO2 to methanol
3 months

A spin-off from a Milan university had a catalyst that worked with a few grams. Investors wanted kilograms. At NODE the startup ran its pilot test with a NEXTCHEM engineer, kept its patent and closed its seed round with the NODE report.

Bio-based plastic
100×

A Spanish startup needed to know what happens when it makes its plastic 100 times bigger. The NODE AI forecast answered in days. With the forecast and the NODE Validated badge the round closed, and a packaging company signed a trial order.

Green hydrogen
20 years

A German startup's electrolyser was validated at NODE, but a refinery wanted a 20-year performance guarantee. MAIRE included it in its offer with its own guarantee and won the contract. The founders earn a royalty on every sale.
